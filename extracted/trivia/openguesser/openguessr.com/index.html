Free GeoGuessr alternative with unlimited playtime and multiplayer. Play GeoGuessr free of charge and learn geography by guessing where you are!

OpenGuessr is like GeoGuessr, but free and unlimited, enabling you to guess the location without a subscription (kostenlos, gratis). The game includes online multiplayer to play with friends, is unblocked, partially open-source, and uses Google Street View. Can you guess the country, or even guess where you are?
